Requirement

Prepare these documents before applying online and before confirming your enrollment slot.

Online Requirements

Exclusive for PUPCET takers

  1. 2x2-inch colored photo with name tag in JPEG format, not more than 300 KB.
  2. Grade 11 report card showing complete name, LRN, first semester GWA, and second semester GWA.

Enrollment Requirements

Exclusive for PUPCET passers

  1. Passed PUP College Entrance Exam Test (PUPCET).
  2. Confirmation requirements after confirming your slot through the PUP iApply Portal.
  3. High school graduate credentials.
  4. High School Card (Form 138).
  5. Form 137-A.
  6. Medical Clearance from PUP Medical Clinic.
  7. Certification of Good Moral Character signed by the high school principal or guidance counselor.
  8. NSO authenticated copy of Birth Certificate or Certificate of Live Birth.
